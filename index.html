Industrial Grading System

Premium Raw Materials for Global Markets

All fish scales undergo meticulous washing, high-temperature sun-drying, and deep organic cleaning to optimize pure gelatin mass extraction yields.

Grade A Premium

Mainly selected transparent, clean freshwater scales with high purity, excellent for premium pharmaceutical gelatin.

  • Moisture: < 12%
  • Impurities: < 0.5%
Grade B Standard

Slightly off-white uniform scales. Perfect for high-demand nutritional supplements and general hydrolyzed collagen.

  • Moisture: 12% - 14%
  • Impurities: < 1.0%
Grade C Industrial

Mixed marine/freshwater scale batches. Highly cost-effective option optimized for agricultural uses or basic technical gelatin.

  • Moisture: < 15%
  • Impurities: < 1.5%
